Lattice coverings and homogeneous covering congruences

John E. Cremona and Peter Koymans

Abstract

We consider the problem of covering $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with a finite number of sublattices of finite index; this may be viewed as a projective, or homogeneous, version of the well-known problem of covering systems of congruences. We give a construction of minimal coverings which produces many, but not all, minimal coverings, prove an inequality relating the size of a covering to the least common multiple of the lattice indices, and determine all minimal coverings with at most$8$ sublattices.
